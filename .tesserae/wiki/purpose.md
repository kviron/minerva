# Purpose — Tesserae

This wiki is the durable knowledge base for **minerva**. It is
Karpathy's three-layer LLM Wiki applied here:

1. **Raw** — files under `data/` (and any other configured sources).
2. **Wiki** — markdown pages under `.tesserae/wiki/<kind>/<slug>.md`.
3. **Schema** — `purpose.md` (this file), `schema.md`, `index.md`, `log.md`.

Every ingest pass reads `purpose.md` to keep the wiki on-mission.
